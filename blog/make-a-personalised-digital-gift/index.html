What makes a CHERMO template feel personal?

Think about the moment just before someone opens a gift. For a birthday, The Locker lets them enter the day and month they were born to discover the letters and photos inside. For a quiet thank-you, The Receipt of Us turns little things you remember into entries on a receipt. The design changes how the story unfolds: one begins with curiosity, the other with recognition.

A personalised album or a digital memory book?

For an album, let the photographs lead. Pick the first day, an ordinary day and a day you still talk about. For a digital memory book, start with a letter and choose pictures to sit beside the words. Both approaches make a personalised gift you can share as a web page.

Explore the digital gift templates to preview the designs and see each price. You supply the photos and message, CHERMO prepares the page, and you share its link after publication.

One photo and a sentence can be the whole gift

If you want something to send today, open the free online Polaroid maker. Pick that one picture, choose a paper you like, and write the detail the camera missed. For the train photo, try “Still my favourite train we never caught.” Download the print and send it with your message. Choosing the memory first saves you from scrolling through your entire gallery looking for a perfect shot.

When the joke takes more than one picture

Use the photo strip maker for a sequence. For a friend who helped you move, pick the pile of boxes, the two of you sitting on the floor, and the takeaway you ate there. In that order, three ordinary photos say more than a collage of your best selfies. A line like “First dinner at mine” gives the strip a place and a time.
