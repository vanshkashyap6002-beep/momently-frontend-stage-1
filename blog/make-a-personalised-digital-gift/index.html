Gifting guide

How to make a personalised digital gift from a photo you already have

Start with the picture you would send them with no explanation. Maybe it is two paper cups on a railway platform. Anyone else sees tea. The two of you remember missing the train.

One photo and a sentence can be the whole gift

If you want something to send today, open the free online Polaroid maker. Pick that one picture, choose a paper you like, and write the detail the camera missed. For the train photo, try “Still my favourite train we never caught.” Download the print and send it with your message. Choosing the memory first saves you from scrolling through your entire gallery looking for a perfect shot.

When the joke takes more than one picture

Use the photo strip maker for a sequence. For a friend who helped you move, pick the pile of boxes, the two of you sitting on the floor, and the takeaway you ate there. In that order, three ordinary photos say more than a collage of your best selfies. A line like “First dinner at mine” gives the strip a place and a time.

When you have a letter to go with the photos

A personalised memory page gives a longer story room. Before choosing a design, put your photos in one folder and write down three things: what happened, what they did, and why you still remember it. Those notes can become your message, even if you usually find writing birthday cards awkward.

Explore the digital gift templates to preview a design and check its price before ordering. CHERMO prepares the page from the details you submit. Once published, you can send its link to your recipient. If the gift is for a particular date, ask about timing before placing the order.

You can cherish an everyday moment without waiting for an occasion. Sometimes the best day to send the railway photo is the day you find it again.
