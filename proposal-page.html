Personalized Digital Proposal Page

Think about how your partner would like to receive a proposal. A quiet page you open together leaves space for a conversation. Decide whether to sit beside them or send the link when you both have time to talk.

Leave room for the question

Begin with a memory that explains why you want a future together. Then describe one thing you hope to do together. Put the question in a separate final sentence so your partner can read it at their own pace.

Which design should I use?

Try The Bloom, listed under Love Story in the catalogue. Look at its opening screen and imagine your recipient seeing the page on a phone. Read your message alongside the preview. If the pictures already tell the story, keep the dedication short and let the last line carry the part only the two of you know.

Price checked on 20 November 2026: ₹19. Confirm the current listing before ordering. Choose The Bloom or compare the templates.

How it works

  1. Choose the design and write the name, title and message as you want them to appear. Check the spelling of names and nicknames before submitting.
  2. Select the photographs you want your recipient to see. Tell CHERMO which image belongs at the beginning and what you would like someone to remember at the end.
  3. Review your order and its price before paying. CHERMO prepares the page using your submitted details. If a date matters, ask about timing before you order.
  4. After publication, open the link yourself before sharing it. Send it directly to your recipient and remember that someone with the link may forward it.

Questions about your gift

Should the title give away the proposal?

A neutral title keeps the surprise until the last lines. Discuss placement with CHERMO before sharing a link that might reveal the question.

How can I try out a caption?

Use free Memory Studio to place a caption beside a photograph and download a keepsake. Reading the words beside the image can help you decide which detail belongs in the longer message.
