Digital Anniversary Gift Page

Choose an ordinary moment you would miss if it disappeared: the station where you met after work, the first breakfast in a new flat or the walk you take every Sunday. An anniversary page can say more with one carefully described routine than with a list of milestones.

Tell the story between two anniversaries

Pair an early photograph with a recent one that echoes it. Describe what has changed between the two pictures and one thing that feels exactly the same.

Which design should I use?

Try The Unfolding, listed under Anniversary in the catalogue. Look at its opening screen and imagine your recipient seeing the page on a phone. Read your message alongside the preview. If the pictures already tell the story, keep the dedication short and let the last line carry the part only the two of you know.

Price checked on 20 November 2026: ₹39. Confirm the current listing before ordering. Choose The Unfolding or compare the templates.

How it works

  1. Choose the design and write the name, title and message as you want them to appear. Check the spelling of names and nicknames before submitting.
  2. Select the photographs you want your recipient to see. Tell CHERMO which image belongs at the beginning and what you would like someone to remember at the end.
  3. Review your order and its price before paying. CHERMO prepares the page using your submitted details. If a date matters, ask about timing before you order.
  4. After publication, open the link yourself before sharing it. Send it directly to your recipient and remember that someone with the link may forward it.

Questions about your gift

What if I forget the exact date?

Use an honest description such as “our first winter together” in your message. Reserve exact dates for moments you can check.

How can I try out a caption?

Use free Memory Studio to place a caption beside a photograph and download a keepsake. Reading the words beside the image can help you decide which detail belongs in the longer message.
