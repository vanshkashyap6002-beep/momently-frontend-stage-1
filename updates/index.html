Website updates

From the first memory pages to the photo booth, here’s what’s changed for the people making keepsakes with CHERMO.

November 14 · Two ways to start

The homepage brings both options together. Choose Make a free Polaroid for a photo print, or Explore Templates for a personal memory page. On phones, the buttons stack so both are easy to reach.

November 13 · See what you’re choosing

Template cards show the designs themselves. Tap an image to open its preview. Homepage pricing follows the catalogue, and the free Studio has its own entry point.

Choosing a booth theme now keeps your strip length. On phones, the shutter sits beneath the viewfinder so you can see the frame as you take the shot.

November 12 · Memory Studio opens

We added a camera, an editing desk and paper collections. Bring a photo from your gallery or take one in the booth, then add your caption and download the print.

October 2026 · Finding a design

We improved template previews and added a sitemap to help search engines discover CHERMO’s public pages.

September 2026 · The first memory pages

Our earliest updates introduced profiles and refined sign-in on desktop and mobile. Later that month, we added a publishing workflow for sharing a finished memory by link.
