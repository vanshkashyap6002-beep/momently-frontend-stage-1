FAQ

What should I know before ordering?

What is a digital memory page?

A digital memory page is a website made from your photos, dates and personal messages that you can share with someone by link.

How much does a personalized memory page cost in India?

Each template has its own one-time price in Indian rupees (₹). Check the template listing for its current price before you order.

Can I share my memory page on WhatsApp?

Yes. Once your page is published, copy its link into a WhatsApp message and send it to your recipient.

How long does it take?

CHERMO prepares the page after you submit your details. Contact us before ordering if you need it for a particular date.

Is my data private?

CHERMO uses the photos and details you submit to prepare your memory. People who receive a published link may be able to view and forward it.

What happens to my page after publishing?

You can open the published page and share its link with your recipient. For a change or an availability question, contact CHERMO with your order reference.
