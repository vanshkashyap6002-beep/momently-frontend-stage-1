Best Friend Memory Page

Start with the story behind a joke, a train journey or a voice note you both remember. Give just enough context to bring back the moment. A familiar nickname can make an opening feel personal even if you have very few photographs together.

Write the detail only your friend knows

For a friend living far away, pair an old photograph of you together with a new one of a familiar place. Write what you would do first if they visited tomorrow. End with the sentence you usually leave unsaid in a quick chat.

Which design should I use?

Try Summer Album, listed under Birthday in the catalogue. Look at its opening screen and imagine your recipient seeing the page on a phone. Read your message alongside the preview. If the pictures already tell the story, keep the dedication short and let the last line carry the part only the two of you know.

How it works

  1. Choose the design and write the name, title and message as you want them to appear. Check the spelling of names and nicknames before submitting.
  2. Select the photographs you want your recipient to see. Tell CHERMO which image belongs at the beginning and what you would like someone to remember at the end.
  3. Review your order and its price before paying. CHERMO prepares the page using your submitted details. If a date matters, ask about timing before you order.
  4. After publication, open the link yourself before sharing it. Send it directly to your recipient and remember that someone with the link may forward it.

Questions about your gift

What if we only have one good photograph?

Tell the story behind that photo. Check the design preview and its media requirements before choosing a layout for your message.

How can I try out a caption?

Use free Memory Studio to place a caption beside a photograph and download a keepsake. Reading the words beside the image can help you decide which detail belongs in the longer message.
