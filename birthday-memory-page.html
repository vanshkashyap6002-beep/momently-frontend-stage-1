Personalized Birthday Memory Page

Think about something the birthday person learned, a place they discovered or an ordinary evening when they made everyone laugh. Start with the photograph that brings that moment back. A single year gives your message a natural beginning and end.

Pick a moment from their year

If friends are contributing, ask each person for one sentence about a specific day. Remove repeated greetings and finish with a wish that belongs to this person, like another trip to the place in the opening picture.

Which design should I use?

Try The Little Box, listed under Birthday in the catalogue. Look at its opening screen and imagine your recipient seeing the page on a phone. Read your message alongside the preview. If the pictures already tell the story, keep the dedication short and let the last line carry the part only the two of you know.

Price checked on 20 November 2026: ₹249. Confirm the current listing before ordering. Choose The Little Box or compare the templates.

How it works

  1. Choose the design and write the name, title and message as you want them to appear. Check the spelling of names and nicknames before submitting.
  2. Select the photographs you want your recipient to see. Tell CHERMO which image belongs at the beginning and what you would like someone to remember at the end.
  3. Review your order and its price before paying. CHERMO prepares the page using your submitted details. If a date matters, ask about timing before you order.
  4. After publication, open the link yourself before sharing it. Send it directly to your recipient and remember that someone with the link may forward it.

Questions about your gift

Can I plan a midnight reveal?

Confirm preparation time before planning the surprise. Once the page is published, you can choose when to send the link.

How can I try out a caption?

Use free Memory Studio to place a caption beside a photograph and download a keepsake. Reading the words beside the image can help you decide which detail belongs in the longer message.
